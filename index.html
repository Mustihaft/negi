Make AI video on your own PC.

AI Video Studio turns an idea into a finished video. It writes the script with a local language model, generates every scene with open video models on your graphics card, and gives you a timeline to cut it together. Your projects stay on your computer.

A real editor, not a prompt box.

Generated clips land in your media library next to your own footage, images and music. Arrange them on video and audio tracks, add titles, fades and cross-dissolves, and step back with undo whenever you like.

From idea to published video.

  1. V1

  2. Write

    Turn an idea into a script and scenes with a local model in Ollama or LM Studio, or any OpenAI-compatible service.

  3. Generate

    Each scene becomes a clip through ComfyUI on your graphics card. Wan 2.1 comes set up and tested.

  4. Edit

    Trim, split and arrange clips on as many tracks as you need, with titles, fades and cross-dissolves.

  5. Export

    Render H.264, H.265, VP9 or ProRes. Every file is checked before it is saved. FFmpeg is included.

  6. Publish

    Post to YouTube, Mastodon, Bluesky, Telegram or Discord, right away or on a schedule.

Install

  1. Download the installer

    About 175 MB. It installs for your Windows user only and needs no administrator rights.

  2. Get past the SmartScreen warning

    The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Start AI Video Studio

    Enter an activation key, or continue with the free Community edition.

To run the app

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Editing and export work on any recent PC

To generate video on your PC

  • An NVIDIA graphics card with about 8 GB of memory (tested on an RTX 3060 Ti)
  • ComfyUI with the Wan 2.1 models, about 9 GB to download. Connect it under Models & providers.
